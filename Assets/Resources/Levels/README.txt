Minecrawler Levels 001-100 – Gentle Progressive Balance v2

Design:
- Level 1: 3 coal, 0 rocks, 0 interior walls.
- Levels 1-5: no interior walls.
- Coal never decreases and rises clearly over the course of the game.
- Boss stages use cleaner arenas with fewer rocks and walls.
- The first level after each boss introduces the next difficulty step.
- Walls are interleaved across top, middle and bottom portions of the map.
- A permanent no-reverse route remains open.
- Every 10th level contains exactly one boss X.
- The key remains dynamic and is not stored in the text files.
